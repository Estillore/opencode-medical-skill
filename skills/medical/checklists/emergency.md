# Emergency prompt (ABCDE + deterioration)

Get help now per facility policy. Do not delay care to finish this.

## ABCDE (facts only)
- A Airway: talking? noisy breathing? obstruction? c-spine concern? [ ]
- B Breathing: rate, effort, SpO2 + device, cyanosis [ ]
- C Circulation: pulse, BP, skin, capillary refill, bleeding [ ]
- D Disability: alert/new confusion/voice/pain/unresponsive; glucose if known [ ]
- E Exposure: temp, rash, wounds, devices [ ]

## Trends (compare to this patient's baseline)
- RR, SpO2 on same O2, HR, BP, mental status, urine output [ ]

## Red flags → help now
- Not protecting airway
- Sudden severe chest pain, neuro change, or severe SOB
- Unresponsive or new severe confusion
- Major bleeding or observed shock
- Facility early-warning trigger already met

## After (fill later)
Time called, what seen, what done, response, who notified, next owner, not assessed.
